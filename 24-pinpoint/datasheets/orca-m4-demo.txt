1 Overview
The ORCA-M4 is a 32-bit microcontroller with an Arm Cortex-M4 style core, 512 KB of flash memory,
128 KB of SRAM, 48 GPIO pins, one 12-bit ADC, two I2C buses, three UARTs, and two SPI buses.
It runs from a single 1.8 V to 3.6 V supply.

3 Pinout and pin description
The ORCA-M4 comes in a 64-pin LQFP package. GPIO pins are grouped into ports A, B and C with
16 pins each. Every GPIO pin can be an input, an output, or an alternate function such as
UART TX, I2C SDA, or a PWM timer channel.
Pin 1 is VBAT. Pins 7, 25 and 49 are VDD. Pins 8, 24 and 48 are VSS (ground).
Pin 60 is BOOT0: hold it high during reset to start the built-in bootloader.
Five-volt tolerant pins are marked FT in table 7. Pins without FT must never see more than VDD + 0.3 V.

6 Electrical characteristics
6.1 Absolute maximum ratings
Stresses above these limits may permanently damage the device.
Supply voltage VDD: -0.3 V to 4.0 V.
Input voltage on FT pins: -0.3 V to 5.5 V. Input voltage on other pins: -0.3 V to VDD + 0.3 V.
Storage temperature: -65 °C to 150 °C. Maximum junction temperature: 125 °C.

6.2 Operating conditions
Normal supply voltage VDD: 1.8 V to 3.6 V. Operating ambient temperature: -40 °C to 85 °C.

=== Page 14 ===
6.3 I/O port characteristics
6.3.16 Output driving current
Each GPIO pin can source or sink up to 25 mA. The sum of the currents sourced by all pins of
one port must not exceed 120 mA, and the total for the whole chip must not exceed 240 mA.
When a pin sinks 8 mA, its output low voltage VOL is at most 0.4 V.
When a pin sources 8 mA, its output high voltage VOH is at least VDD - 0.4 V.
Internal pull-up and pull-down resistors are about 40 kOhm.
Figure 12 shows the GPIO source and sink limits.

7 Clocks
7.1 Clock sources
The internal RC oscillator (HSI) runs at 16 MHz with 1 % accuracy at 25 °C.
An external crystal (HSE) from 4 MHz to 26 MHz can be connected to pins OSC_IN and OSC_OUT.
A 32.768 kHz crystal can drive the real-time clock.
7.2 PLL
The PLL can multiply the HSI or HSE clock. The maximum system clock is 168 MHz.

=== Page 31 ===
9 Analog to digital converter
9.1 ADC characteristics
The ADC has 12-bit resolution and up to 16 input channels on pins PA0 to PA7 and PB0 to PB7.
The maximum sampling rate is 2.4 MSPS (million samples per second).
The input range is 0 V to VREF+. VREF+ must be between 1.8 V and VDD.
Typical total unadjusted error is ±2 LSB.

=== Page 38 ===
11 I2C interface
11.1 I2C characteristics
Both I2C buses support standard mode (100 kHz), fast mode (400 kHz), and fast mode plus (1 MHz).
7-bit and 10-bit addressing are supported. External pull-up resistors are required on SDA and SCL;
4.7 kOhm is typical at 100 kHz and 2.2 kOhm at 400 kHz.

13 Power modes
13.1 Current consumption
Run mode at 168 MHz: 38 mA typical. Sleep mode: 9 mA. Stop mode: 12 µA.
Standby mode: 2.1 µA with the real-time clock off. Wake-up from standby takes about 120 µs.
